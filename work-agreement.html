Section 01

Project Overview

This Statement of Work ("Agreement") outlines the scope, deliverables, timeline, and terms for the design, development, and deployment of a comprehensive Digital Clinic Ecosystem. The ecosystem encompasses a patient-facing mobile application (iOS & Android), a surgeon & assistant admin dashboard (Web), a patient-facing web portal, and all supporting backend infrastructure and integrations described herein.

Both parties agree that this document constitutes the complete understanding of the project scope. Any features, modules, or integrations not explicitly listed in this Agreement are considered out of scope and will require a separate change order.

Section 02

Scope of Work — Deliverables

1 Authentication & Onboarding

  • Passwordless Login: OTP-based login via SMS/WhatsApp for frictionless entry.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Distinct login portals for Surgeons, Assistants, and Patients.
  • Smart Patient Onboarding: Digital intake forms capturing basic medical history and consent during initial signup.

2 Patient Mobile App (iOS & Android)

  • Dashboard & Unified Hub: Home screen displaying upcoming appointments, current prescriptions, and active subscription status.
  • VIP Subscription Portal: Tiered membership checkout (integrated with Stripe/Razorpay) with auto-renewal.
  • Digital VIP Care Card: A premium, glassmorphism-styled digital membership card with a scannable patient QR code.
  • Secure Medical Vault: Camera integration to snap and store lab reports, X-rays, and past prescriptions.
  • Triage Chat System: Asynchronous messaging with automated screening questions before the message reaches the surgeon.
  • Telemedicine Video Consults: Integrated secure video calling for remote check-ups.
  • "Health Drop" Content Feed: A continuous, scrollable feed of the surgeon's micro-videos and health tips.
  • Automated Push Reminders: System-triggered alerts for medication schedules, follow-up bookings, and upcoming local camps.
  • 1-Click Appointment Booking: Calendar sync to book available in-clinic or virtual slots.
  • Referral Engine: Unique invite codes allowing patients to earn discounts by referring friends.

3 Surgeon & Assistant Admin Dashboard (Web)

  • Unified Patient CRM: Centralized database to search, filter, and view full patient histories and uploaded vault documents.
  • 1-Click Digital Prescriptions: Dropdown-driven Rx generator that outputs a branded, e-signed PDF.
  • WhatsApp Auto-Delivery: Instant dispatch of generated PDF prescriptions directly to the patient's WhatsApp.
  • "Office Hours" Chat Manager: Inbox that automatically sends "out of office" replies when patients message outside clinic hours.
  • Smart Reply Snippets: Pre-saved text blocks for one-click answers to common post-op or diet queries.
  • Voice-to-Text Clinical Notes: Microphone integration to dictate consultation notes directly into the CRM.
  • Revenue & MRR Analytics: Visual charts tracking subscription growth, one-off payments, and total revenue.
  • "Camp Mode" Offline Sync: Ability to log new rural patient data offline on a tablet, which auto-syncs to the server when Wi-Fi is restored.
  • Content Management System (CMS): Simple upload portal to push new videos or announcements to the patient app.
  • Automated Monthly Practice Reports: An automatic email sent on the 1st of the month summarizing clinic growth and metrics.

4 Patient-Facing Web Portal & Growth Engine

  • "Tech-Luxury" Landing Page: Premium, high-trust aesthetic showcasing the surgeon's authority.
  • Camp Locator & Pre-Registration: Interactive map and signup form to capture leads for upcoming rural medical camps.
  • Web Booking Engine: Browser-based appointment scheduling for non-app users.
  • Masterclass Video Library: A responsive web gallery hosting long-form educational medical content.
  • Automated Review Funnel: System that automatically requests a Google Review via SMS 24 hours after a successful 5-star consultation.
